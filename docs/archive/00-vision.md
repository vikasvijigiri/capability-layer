# 00 — Vision & Design Principles

## Vision
To build an Intelligence Operating System (UAIOS) that orchestrates
reasoning, planning, learning and execution across models, tools and memories
— enabling repeatable, verifiable, and safe autonomous workflows.

## Design Principles
- Modularity: small, testable subsystems.
- Observability: measurable signals and traces.
- Economy: optimize token, time, and cost.
- Safety: validation, governance, and access controls.
- Reusability: promote successful patterns as Blueprints.

## High-level Goals
- Reliable automation for multi-step tasks.
- Continuous learning from outcomes.
- Deterministic validation and audit trails.
